What is

The Well-Kept Habitat?

The Well-Kept Habitat explores how elegant landscapes can support biodiversity, strengthen ecology, and bring people together.

Through gardens, stories, gatherings, local knowledge, and partnerships, TWKH creates room for cultivated spaces to do more—without giving up the elegance that makes people care for them.

The big idea

Elegant. Useful. Connected.

✦

Elegant

⬡

◌

Connected

Why TWKH exists

What if the spaces we already cultivate could do more?

Our communities devote enormous amounts of land, energy, money, and attention to the landscapes around our homes and neighborhoods.

TWKH begins with a simple premise: ecological usefulness and elegance do not have to compete. A well-kept landscape can be cultivated, ecologically meaningful, and part of a stronger community.
